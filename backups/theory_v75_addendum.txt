The composite score is a better filter than any individual gate.

FORMULA (approximate, ECN pts notation):
  score = 0.0305 * (ATR_z) + 0.0069 * (r_z) - 0.0132 * (qc_z) + 0.1032

of each coefficient is robust (ATR+, r+, qc-) but magnitudes should not be
trusted for live use without OOS validation.

rather than trying to implement a continuous score in live trading.
