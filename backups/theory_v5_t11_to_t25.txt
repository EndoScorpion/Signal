## Theory 25 — T7 DOW-Filtered (Key Finding)

**Concept:** T7 (ATR compression breakout 13-17 UTC) restricted to Mon+Tue+Wed only.

**Discovery path:**
1. T7 all-days: PF 2.42, n=40
2. DOW breakdown of T7: Mon PF 4.31, Tue PF 3.09, Wed PF 3.29 vs Thu PF 1.56, Fri PF 1.38
3. T25 Mon+Tue+Wed: **PF 3.41**, n=23

**Results:**
- 2024: n=4, WR=50%, PF=1.08
- 2025: n=9, WR=77.8%, PF=8.63
- 2026: n=10, WR=70%, PF=3.51

**Robustness:** ATR rank <20 is load-bearing (rank<25 drops to PF 1.38). Breakout multiplier stable across 0.2–0.4x range.

**Why Thu+Fri weakens T7:**
- Thursday: pre-NFP positioning. Big players pre-position for Friday NFP — this creates directional flows that OVERRIDE compression breakout signals. Breakout fires into a wall of pre-positioned risk.
- Friday: end-of-week position unwinding. Institutional risk reduction means less follow-through after breakouts. Market closes early psychologically.
- Mon+Tue+Wed: pure institutional flow. No macro event overhang, full commitment to moves.

**Parameters:**
- Instrument: EURUSD H1 (FXOpen ECN)
- Filter: ATR rank < 20th percentile for 3+ consecutive bars
- Trigger: bar breaks prior 10-bar H/L by > 0.2×ATR14
- Time gate: 13:00-17:00 UTC
- DOW gate: Mon, Tue, Wed only (skip Thu, Fri)
- Entry: at close of trigger bar
- Stop: 1.5×ATR14; Target: 1.5×ATR14 (1:1 R:R)
- Max hold: 4 bars

**Caution:** n=23 over 2.5 years (~9/yr). Needs 5yr validation per CLAUDE.md rule. 2024 borderline (n=4, PF 1.08).

## What Didn't Work (Key Learnings)

1. **Prior week levels (T11):** Weekly H/L breakouts on Monday = PF 0.78. The level is too well-known; gets faded.
2. **Momentum continuation (T13, T23):** 3-bar momentum and spike bar fades both PF ~0.83-0.90. H1 EURUSD is not a trend-continuation instrument at the intraday level.
3. **Gap fades (T14):** London open gaps get filled ~50% but size of losses on non-fills exactly cancels gains. Symmetric outcome.
4. **NYfade of London extension (T16-17):** Consistent signal direction (PF 1.1+) but insufficient edge. The mean-reversion exists but is too small to survive costs.
5. **RSI divergence (T20):** Asia RSI extreme failed badly (PF 0.51). RSI on EURUSD H1 is noise, not a signal.
6. **T3 tighter filters (T21):** Requiring stronger London move reduces n and PF — T3 is already optimally calibrated.
7. **T7+T3 confluence (T22):** Only 4 joint trades — signals are too rare to combine.
