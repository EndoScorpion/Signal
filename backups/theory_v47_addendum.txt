  7.4pts → +0.1032%   (rank=16.3)
  8.3pts → +0.1167%   (rank=17.9)
  9.2pts → +0.1299%   (rank=16.7)
  9.9pts → +0.1399%   (rank=15.5)
  11.3pts → +0.1618%  (rank=14.7)

  ATR bins:
  5-7pts:  n=3, avg=+0.0730%
  7-10pts: n=6, avg=+0.1151%
  >10pts:  n=1, avg=+0.1618%

WHY THIS MAKES SENSE:
The PnL formula is: (en - ex) - COST, where exit is ±1.5×ATR from entry.
A 1.5×ATR target at ATR=6pts is 9pts absolute. At ATR=11pts it's 16.5pts.
In price terms: target = 1.5×ATR, and the PnL% = 1.5×ATR / entry_price.
Since EURUSD is around 1.05-1.10, a 1pt ATR produces ~0.0143% per ATR unit.
More ATR = larger absolute move to target = higher PnL%.

HOWEVER: This creates a potential circularity — higher ATR setups produce more
points of PnL by construction (target = 1.5×ATR). The REAL question is whether
higher ATR setups hit the target MORE OFTEN. At WR=100%, all targets are hit —
so the correlation is partially mechanical.

IMPORTANT CAVEAT: ATR is already represented in the ATR rank filter (rank<20).
The rank filter ensures low percentile of ATR, but within that band the absolute
level varies. Higher ATR within the rank<20 band means we're in a period of
HIGHER absolute volatility (e.g., 2024 year-end vs mid-2026). The December 2024
trades (ATR 9-11pts) naturally produced more PnL than mid-2026 trades (ATR 5-7pts).

FILTER POTENTIAL:
  ATR > 7pts: n=7, avg=+0.1151%  (+7% vs base)
  ATR > 8pts: n=3, avg=+0.1361%  (+27% vs base)  [2024-12-10, 2024-12-18, 2026-03-30]
  (Note: ATR>8pts has only 3 trades — same small-n caution applies)

CONCLUSION: T323 MAJOR FINDING. ATR absolute value has +0.912 correlation with
PnL — highest in the study. However, this is PARTIALLY MECHANICAL (larger ATR →
larger PnL% by construction of fixed 1.5×ATR target). The non-mechanical portion
is that higher-ATR periods may produce more reliable setups. ATR absolute should
be added to the composite score to capture this effect, distinguishing it from ATR
rank (which measures compression level, not absolute magnitude).

NEW COMPOSITE CANDIDATE (V6):
  Add ATR absolute as a normalized component. Normalization: (atr_abs - min_threshold) / range.
  The two effects (ATR rank = compression, ATR absolute = magnitude) are genuinely different.
