  momentum state that attracts BOTH an 08 UTC exhaustion bounce (S2) AND a 13-14 UTC
  continuation short (S1). The S2 bounce is weaker on these days because the bearish
  trend is stronger — the exhaustion bounce is shallower.

  However: ALL 3 same-day S2 signals are still profitable (all winners), so the
  weaker quality is not a signal quality problem, just a natural relationship.
