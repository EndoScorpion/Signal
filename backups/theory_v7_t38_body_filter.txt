## Key Finding: Breakout Bar Body Size as Conviction Proxy

**Discovery path:**
1. T25 has 23 trades with PF=3.41
2. Checking body size (|close-open|) of winners vs losers:
   - **Winners avg body/ATR: 0.701**
   - **Losers avg body/ATR: 0.210**
3. Extremely strong discriminator — losers are doji/small-body breakouts
4. Tested body>0.3xATR filter across all DOW (T38a): **n=28, PF=3.21**

**Why large body = conviction:**
- ATR compression means market participants have withdrawn. When compression breaks, the move must be decisive — a bar that closes far from its open (large body) represents directional commitment, not a fakeout.
- A small-body breakout bar that just barely exceeds the 10-bar high/low = thin order flow, single price probe. Market doesn't back it up → reverses.
- Large body = institutions participated, moved price, held it. This is the signal the breakout is real.
- This is a proxy for volume conviction (no volume data in H1 ECN feed).

## Mechanism Summary

T7 → T25 → T38a reveals a single mechanism evolving:

1. **T7 (base):** ATR compression + 13-17 UTC + breakout → edge exists
2. **T25 (DOW filter):** Remove Thu/Fri pre-positioning noise → edge stronger

All three filters target the SAME phenomenon from different angles: **real institutional participation during peak NY/London overlap liquidity**. DOW and body size are two independent proxies for this.
