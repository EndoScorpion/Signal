## Key Finding: T75 — Wick-Poke Shorts (New Strongest Signal)

**Discovery path from T72:**
T72 tested whether the T59 short trigger bar closed BELOW the 10-bar low vs just wicked below:
- Full close below lo10 (close < lo10): n=10, WR=70%, PF=2.85
- **Wick-poke only (close >= lo10, but low < lo10 - 0.2×ATR): n=8, WR=87.5%, PF=21.14**

**The wick-poke filter is the strongest discriminant found so far.**

### Mechanism
A wick-poke short means:
1. Price wicked below the 10-bar support level (ATR-compressed range broke down)
2. BUT the bar CLOSED back above the 10-bar low — buyers defended and pushed price back
3. This "defense" is temporary and unsustainable (bearish continuation follows)

**Why this is the stronger signal:** The wick poke shows EUR buyers tried to defend support and STILL failed to push price up. The close-back is a short trap. Next bars continue lower. In contrast, a full close below lo10 = obvious breakdown = sometimes already priced in and chased.
