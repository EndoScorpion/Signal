## Key Finding: CCI is Implicit in T25

**T33 discovery:** When CCI(14) gate (>100 aligned with breakout direction) applied to T25:
- 21 of 23 T25 trades ALREADY have CCI>100 aligned with breakout
- 1 trade has CCI in neutral zone (-100 to +100) — this trade is a loser
- 0 trades have CCI counter-aligned

**Conclusion:** ATR compression breakouts during the 13-17 UTC window are structurally correlated with CCI extremes. The compression itself creates the CCI condition. CCI gate adds marginal value (PF 3.41→3.67) but is NOT an independent filter.

**Why this happens:** When price compresses into an ATR rank <20th pct for 3+ bars during peak liquidity, market participants who read momentum indicators like CCI are simultaneously building up extreme readings. The breakout then fires at the precise moment both conditions are satisfied — they co-occur by construction.

## Theory 35 — London Momentum Continuation Mon-Wed

**Concept:** If London 07-13 UTC moves >0.5×ATR directionally, follow that direction 13-17 UTC.

**Results:** n=382, WR=51%, PF=0.90. Flat across all thresholds (0.3x, 0.5x, 0.8x, 1.0x, 1.5xATR all produce PF 0.80-0.93).

**REJECTED:** London momentum does NOT continue into NY on EURUSD. The NY session is an independent institutional event, not a London continuation. This was partially explored in T16/T17 (NY fade, PF 1.10) — that was slightly better than this continuation test, confirming NY is genuinely mean-reverting vs London but not enough edge to trade.

## Theory 27 — T3 + Weekly Open Direction Filter

**Concept:** T3 (Mon+Thu session divergence) only when London direction aligns with the current week's open-to-now trend.

**Results — aligned with weekly trend:** n=18, WR=50%, PF=3.46
**Results — fighting weekly trend:** n=47, WR=40.4%, PF=1.72

**Learning:** The weekly open filter cuts T3 from n=65 to n=18 — too aggressive. The aligned group has better PF (3.46 vs 2.17) but n is too small. The reject group (fighting weekly trend, n=47) still has PF=1.72 — not worthless.

**BORDERLINE:** The weekly trend alignment direction is a real effect but the filter is too aggressive (cuts 72% of trades). Needs 5yr data to confirm with adequate n.

## What This Batch Taught

1. **CCI is not independent of ATR compression breakouts.** 21/23 T25 trades already satisfy CCI>100. The compression creates the CCI extreme — they're two readings of the same phenomenon.
2. **London momentum does not continue into NY.** 382 trade sample, PF=0.90. NY = independent session. T7/T25's mechanism is specifically about *compression during* NY overlap, not continuation from London.
3. **ATR band tightening reduces n without improving PF.** T25's <20th pct threshold is optimal. Narrower bands (5-15, <10) reduce n and add noise.
4. **Weekly open direction is a real effect on T3.** But cuts 72% of trades — needs more data before acting.
5. **Price near 20-bar extreme during compression (T37) is interesting.** n=12 is too thin but the concept has theoretical merit: breakouts from multi-period extremes during peak liquidity carry more conviction.
