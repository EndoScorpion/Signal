This seems counterintuitive (we expect compression before breakout), but it makes sense:
- A large 10-bar range WITH quiet recent bars (qc>=3) means: recent consolidation after
  a big move. The 10-bar range captures the size of the prior SWING, not just the quiet.
- The quiet bars (qc=3-6) capture the recent compression; range10 captures the swing size.
- Large swing → more momentum available for the breakout continuation.

VERDICT: range10 is positively correlated with pnl but CANNOT be used as a minimum gate.
It HURTS when used as a maximum (drops best trades). It is a soft indicator:
larger 10-bar range (more prior movement) = better trades.
The current qc metric already partially captures this. Adding range10 as a gate:
  Do NOT add range10<=X as a filter — it removes the best trades.
  Soft interpretation: range10 >= 4.0 is a positive indicator (n=3 all strong).
