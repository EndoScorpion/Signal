=== SARA / PRV DEFINITIONS ===

SARA (Stop-And-Reverse Awareness):
   SARA = min(abs(close - swing_high_1ATR), abs(close - swing_low_1ATR)) / ATR(14)
   - Lookback: 20 bars for swing detection
   - Low SARA = price is near a swing high/low = near stop-hunt zone = risky entry
   - Used INVERTED in denominator: near zone = higher denominator = lower REVOLT score
   - Weight in denominator: 5%

PRV (Price Reversion Vector):
   PRV = (close - SMA(20)) / ATR(14)
   - Positive PRV = price above 20-bar mean = reversion pull downward (sell assist)
   - Negative PRV = price below 20-bar mean = reversion pull upward (buy assist)
   - Only POSITIVE contribution counted (PRV_pos = max(PRV, 0))
   - Weight in numerator: 10%
