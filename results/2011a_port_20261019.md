## Findings

### What works from the 2011a EA
1. **SLP+OVB gates consistently improve PF** across all variants (+0.07–0.19 PF lift vs base).  
   This is the most portable idea: the loss taxonomy gates are real.

2. **MKT-BB-LO-T38l+gates** (London hours 07–16 + both gates): n=86, PF=1.39, WR=57%, DD=-0.67%.  
   Best absolute numbers but n<30 in many years — not yet a survivor.

3. **MKT-BB-T35l+SLP+OVB** is the broadest passing variant:  
   FXOpen PF=1.22 (all 3 years positive); TwelveData PF=1.11 (4/7 years fail — not consistent enough).

### What the EA's 98% WR cannot port
- No stop loss: the EA held losers indefinitely. In 2011's EUR trending market this worked.  
  Adding 1.5×ATR stop drops WR to ~47–57% — correct for modern data, but the "98%" disappears.
- Regime-specific: 2011 EURUSD was low-vol trending (EMA slope always positive). Gates work because the EA implicitly required this too.

### Recommended gate definitions (share with ratio correlation thread)
```
Gate A (Neg/Weak SLP): ema120.diff(5) >= -0.1 * atr14  # flat or positive trend
Gate B (OVB): stoch_k(5,3) < 85 AND NOT (mom15 < mom15_prev AND mom15_prev > 0)
```

### Most worth carrying forward
**The two loss-prevention gates (Gate A + Gate B) applied to any LONG mean-reversion rule.**  
They add PF lift without needing the 2011 data, and they match the loss taxonomy exactly.  
MKT-BB-LO-T38l+gates needs more data (n=86/7yr) before it can be a survivor.
