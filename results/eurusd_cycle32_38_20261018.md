## C35: 08:00 Entry (bear07 confirmation)

| Strategy | n | PF | avg% | win% | 2024 PF | 2025 PF | 2026 PF |
|----------|---|----|------|------|---------|---------|---------|
| v2.3 base (07:00 ALL) | 32 | 5.38 | +0.186% | 43.8% | 3.86 | 7.06 | 6.05 |
| bear07 subset (07:00 entry) | 19 | 10.33 | +0.319% | 47.4% | 7.27 | inf | 10.82 |
| bear07 subset (08:00 entry) | 19 | 5.17 | +0.163% | 47.4% | 6.35 | 5.46 | 4.81 |

**Key insight:** Entering at 07:00 OPEN on bear07 trades is dramatically better (PF=10.33) than waiting for 08:00 (PF=5.17). The 07:00 open provides superior fill — the fade works best captured immediately at entry.

**Practical implication for v2.4:** The bear07 filter is a POST-HOC filter — you can only confirm it after the 07:00 bar closes at 08:00. The practical v2.4 implementation would be: enter at 07:00 open always, then EXIT EARLY at 08:00 if the 07:00 bar closed BULLISH (i.e., skip the 12-13 trades where 07:00 was bullish). n=19, PF=10.33. **Pending — needs n≥30.**
