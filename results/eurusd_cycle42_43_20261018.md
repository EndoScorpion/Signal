**Key finding:** When Asia has been bullish for 0–1 of the previous 3 days (fresh move), the fade produces PF=8.53 with ALL YEARS positive and consistent. When Asia has been bullish 2-3 of the previous 3 days (persistent bull run), PF drops to 2.72 with 2024 losing (PF=0.63).

**Structural interpretation:** A single-day Asia spike into London is a classic mean-reversion setup (short-term extension). A multi-day Asia bull run is more likely a genuine trend change — the fade is fighting momentum and fails more often.

**The "0 prior up days" result** (n=4, PF=inf, 100% win rate) is the extreme case: Asia is up today but was DOWN all 3 prior days. This is the purest spike/reversal setup. n=4 is too small to trust but theoretically strongest.

**Verdict:** 0-1 prior up days (n=16, PF=8.53) is structurally compelling and all-years consistent. n=16 < 30. **Pending candidate — strong structural backing.**
