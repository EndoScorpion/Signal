NFP NEWS STRADDLE - EURUSD M5, FXOpen costs 11 pts + optional 10 pts news slippage (updated 2026-10-10)
Dates: forexfactory.com is not reachable from here, so release days = first Friday of each month (the usual NFP rule;
a few months release on the 2nd Friday, which this misses). Server time: the biggest first-Friday M5 spike is at 15:30
server time all year (average 351 pts vs 188 for the next bar), so the server shifts with US daylight time. 110 NFP days in data.
Setup: at the close of the 15:25 bar, buy stop at price + d and sell stop at price - d, OCO, expiry 10/30/60 min.
If both levels are hit inside the spike bar, the worst case (full stop loss) is assumed. Control = the same straddle at a
quiet hour on the same Fridays. Variants: 252 (7 distances incl. 1-3x ATR, 3 expiries, 6 exits, 2 slippage levels).
Years: 2011, 2012, 2017 (partial), 2019, 2020 (partial), 2021. Only 5-12 NFP trades per year, so every number is noisy.

RESULT: no variant is positive in all six years (0 of 252). No win to report.
